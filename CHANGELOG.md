## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Class trainer spells, pet/demon abilities, hunter beast browser, weapon skills, trainer locations with waypoints, profession trainer recipes and other recipes — all inside the spellbook and professions windows.
- Compact card layout: two spells per row with a colored status edge, level and price meta line.
- Sentinel look: flat dark panels, hairline borders, gold accent, custom checkboxes, dropdowns, buttons, scroll bars, windows and minimap button.
- Settings: minimap button, spell ranks (auto ranks), price display, per-view tab toggles, layout mode, language selection, about section.
- 11 client languages: enUS, deDE, esES, esMX, frFR, itIT, koKR, ptBR, ruRU, zhCN, zhTW.
- Single-root database (`SentinelMentorDB`) covering classes, pets, professions (skills + recipes), trainer locations, weapon skills and hunter beasts.
- Copyright module with integrity check (`Core/Copyright.lua`).
- Dungeon journal integrated: bosses, loot, quests, entrances, routes, per-dungeon themed artwork, favorites, LFG button, map markers, settings panel and addon compartment entry.
